# My agent: Chef Gemini (Personal Chef & Meal Architect)
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps home cooks plan meals, discover recipes, and scale cooking instructions with a catalog of curated recipes and pantry ingredients.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers dietary restrictions (e.g., allergies, vegan/keto), skill level, available pantry staples, and flavor preferences across sessions.
- Tools: Looks up recipes by ingredients, generates grocery shopping lists, and performs pantry inventory checks.
- Catalog/UI: Curated recipe catalog and meal plans rendered as rich A2UI cards and ingredient tables.
- Image gen: Generates visual dish previews and presentation/plating concepts for custom or modified recipes.
- Sandbox: Computes nutritional breakdowns, ingredient ratio scaling, and metric/imperial unit conversions.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for recipe scaling and nutrition calculations, external grocery or nutrition APIs.
